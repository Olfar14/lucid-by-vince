Question of the Month
When markets hit all-time highs and consumer sentiment hits record lows — who is right?
Markets price the future; sentiment measures the present. S&P at 7,580 ATH. UMich at 44.8 record low. One of them is badly wrong — or both are, for different reasons.
Cognitive Bias of the Month
Apophenia — seeing patterns where none exist
After every geopolitical shock, analysts construct inevitability narratives. In hindsight everything looks causal. In the moment, it was noise. The bias that makes markets predictable only in retrospect.

Word you should know
Imaginal
Adjective · from Latin imaginalis · depth psychology
Relating to the imagination as a distinct cognitive space — images that are neither purely literal nor purely abstract. In a month where market "reality" and consumer "reality" violently diverged, imaginal thinking — holding two contradictory truths simultaneously — may be the most useful cognitive skill available.
Album · 59 years ago today
Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band
The Beatles · Released June 1, 1967
On June 1, 1967, the Beatles released the album widely considered the most influential in recorded history. It introduced the concept album, the studio as instrument, and the idea that pop music could carry philosophical weight. In a month that felt simultaneously euphoric and terrified, Sgt. Pepper remains the definitive document of exactly that tension.
